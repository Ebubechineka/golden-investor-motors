Our story

A dealership built on inspection, not sales talk

Golden Investor Motors exists because buying a used car in Nigeria should not feel like a gamble. We buy carefully, inspect properly, document everything, and describe cars the way we would want them described to us.

How we work

Three things we refuse to do

Sell a car we have not driven. Every vehicle goes through our workshop before listing. If a fault cannot be fixed economically, the car does not reach the showroom.

Describe a car better than it is. Mileage is verified, condition is stated plainly, and photographs show the actual vehicle. What you read is what you see.

Rush a handover. Documents are checked and transferred properly before keys change hands, even when a buyer is in a hurry.

The inspection

What a 150-point check actually covers

Mechanical

Engine compression and leaks, gearbox behaviour through all gears, clutch or torque converter, differentials, exhaust integrity.

Chassis & suspension

Shock absorbers, bushings, control arms, wheel bearings, steering play, alignment, and a check for accident repair or chassis damage.

Body & paint

Panel alignment, paint depth readings to detect respray, rust in known problem areas, glass, seals and underbody condition.

Documents & history

Customs papers, chassis and engine number match, mileage plausibility, service records, and ownership chain where available.

Talk to someone who has driven the car

Ask about any vehicle in stock and we will tell you what we found — including the things that are not perfect.
